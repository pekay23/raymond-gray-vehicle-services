Overview

Core Service Pillars

The Raymond Gray Vehicle Services platform provides a comprehensive suite of automotive services.

01 · Independent EV Sales & Advisory

Independent EV Sales & Advisory

  • Curated Procurement: Brand-agnostic vehicle procurement ensuring the right fit for the client.
  • Cost & Range Analysis: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) projections and real-world range assessments rather than manufacturer claims.
  • End-to-End Setup: Complete delivery coordination and charging setup for new vehicles.
02 · Structured EV & ICE Maintenance

Structured EV & ICE Maintenance

  • Preventive Focus: Manufacturer-aligned preventive maintenance.
  • Specialized EV Care: Detailed battery health diagnostics, regenerative braking system checks, and critical firmware updates.
  • Traditional ICE Care: Standard servicing, exhaust and emissions system care, drivetrain, and mechanical diagnostics.
03 · Fleet Management Integration

Fleet Management Integration

  • Visibility & Control: GPS-enabled fleet tracking, driver behavior monitoring, and detailed journey logging.
  • Maintenance Scheduling: Automated Preventive Planned Maintenance (PPM) calendars.
  • Reporting: Fuel/energy cost reporting and monthly fleet health summaries.
04 · Charging Infrastructure

Charging Infrastructure

  • End-to-End Management: Full lifecycle management from initial site power assessments to AC/DC rapid charger installation.
  • Smart Infrastructure: Integration of smart charging load management to utilize off-peak energy rates.
  • Maintenance: Ongoing maintenance and fault management for installed chargers.
