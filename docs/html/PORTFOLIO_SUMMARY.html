02 · Long description (case study body, ~250 words)

Long description (case study body, ~250 words)

Raymond Gray's vehicle services division needed its own identity. The service offering — independent EV sales and advisory, structured EV & ICE maintenance, fleet management, and charging infrastructure — was buried inside the main Raymond Gray Platform site under /services/vehicle-repairs. It shared a page with the rest of the IFM platform and had no dedicated booking flow, no standalone brand surface, and no way to reach corporate fleet clients or individual EV owners without sending them through the full company site.

I built Raymond Gray Vehicle Services as a standalone Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript microsite, extracted from the main platform and deployed at its own subdomain. The site is public marketing pages only — no auth, no database — with six routes: / (home), /book, /faqs, /privacy, /terms, plus generated /sitemap.xml and /robots.txt.

The non-obvious work is in the booking wizard and component architecture. The four-step /book wizard collects vehicle details (make, model, reg, odometer), a structured maintenance tier (A1 Minor → B2 Complete Plan, General Repairs, or Diagnostics with free-text fault description), service logistics (workshop drop-off, home call, or towing — the latter triggering an interactive Leaflet-powered location picker that captures precise lat/long and street address), and contact & confirmation (WhatsApp + email, dynamic review summary, authorization checkbox). Bot submissions under three seconds are silently dropped. The form POSTs to the main platform's /api/vehicle-bookings endpoint and falls back to a pre-filled WhatsApp message if the API is unavailable, ensuring the lead is never lost.

The component kit is a reusable section primitive library (PageHero, SplitFeature, PillarRows, CalloutSlab, StatStrip, ProcessTimeline, ImageBand, CarouselSection, CapabilityIndex) assembled on a Tailwind v4 design system of ~200 --rg-* brand tokens. Two animation engines run in separate lanes: Framer Motion for component-level enter/exit and layout animations, and GSAP + ScrollTrigger for scroll-driven scrubs, pinning, and parallax — both respecting prefers-reduced-motion. Navigation includes a scroll-reactive TopNav, a sticky SectionNav that auto-discovers sections via data-screen-label and collapses to a dropdown on mobile, and a floating "Back to Top" button that appears past 500px scroll.

Outcome: the vehicle services offering now has a standalone brand surface with a structured booking flow, independent deployment, and a clear separation of concerns from the main IFM platform — with the old /services/vehicle-repairs page redirecting permanently to the subdomain.
